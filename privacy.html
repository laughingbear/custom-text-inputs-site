Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Data Collection

Custom Text Inputs does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

Local Storage

Your snippets are stored only in your browser’s local storage (and Chrome sync storage if you are signed in and sync is enabled). This data stays on your device and in your Google account’s Chrome sync, if applicable. We never receive or have access to your snippets.

Exports

If you choose to export your snippets, the file is created and downloaded entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Analytics & Tracking

This extension and this website contain no analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the contact information listed on the Chrome Web Store listing.
